Professor

신동훈Donghoon Shin, Ph.D.

Associate Professor · Artificial Intelligence Engineering

Dr. Donghoon Shin is an Associate Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence Engineering (Intelligent Control Systems Major) at Korea Maritime & Ocean University (KMOU), where he currently serves as Major Coordinator of the Intelligent Control Systems Major, after serving as Chair of the Department of AI Engineering (2025–2026). He directs RiMS (Research in Intelligent Mobility Systems), an interdisciplinary lab developing learning-based and Physical AI methods that bridge data-driven models with the physical dynamics of real-world machines — spanning autonomous vehicles, robotics, and maritime autonomous systems.

Professional Experience

  • Director of RiMS Lab., Future Mobility Technology Center, Seoul National University
  • Assistant Professor, Mechanical Systems Engineering, Sookmyung Women's University
  • Development Engineer, Advanced Driver Assistance Systems, Mercedes-Benz Research & Development
  • Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering, Korea Naval Academy (Navy Officer)

Education

  • Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, Seoul National University
  • B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, University of Minnesota Twin Cities
