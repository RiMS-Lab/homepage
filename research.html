Research Interests

AI based Intelligent Mobility Dynamics and Control, including:

-          AI-Powered Hybrid Control

-          Indoor/Outdoor Localization

-          Human-Like Naturalistic Driver Model using Deep Learning

-          Radar Data based Moving Target Perception and Detection using Deep Learning

-          Cloud/Edge and C-V2X (Vehicle to Everything) based Vehicle Safety Control

-          Deep Reinforcement Learning for Motion Planning of Autonomous Vehicles

-          Deep Learning Sensor Fusion
